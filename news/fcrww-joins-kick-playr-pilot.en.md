FC Rot-Weiss Winikon (FCRWW) has decided to join the kick-playr pilot.

## Built around club life

The app was originally designed and built exclusively for FC Rot-Weiss Winikon (FCRWW) in Uster. That original FCRWW app now forms the foundation of the kick-playr.ch app, bringing the experience of a real club to its further development.

## Free for FCRWW. Forever.

FCRWW will remain a free customer of kick-playr.ch forever, in recognition of its role as the club for which the app was originally built.

## Less admin. More football.

The pilot connects the project with everyday club experience and helps inform its next steps.
